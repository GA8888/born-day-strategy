Bulletproof Rules

Avoid trading premarket/aftermarket hours.

Why? It's just not the strongest and most predictable sessions.

Use RTH EMA's always.

Why? Because the ETH EMA's tend to be inconsistent especially at NY session open.

Control the obsession to charts and practicing.

Why? Trading causes mental fatigue — the longer you sit at a chart, the more you start to make up things that aren't present, and if things aren't there you tend to make moves that don't work out.

Only trade the first 2 hours of NY session.

Why? That's the strongest and most predictable time to trade, and it's my personal strongest time.

Ease into positions at open or anytime.

Why? Positions and analysis are not always right, so easing in with 1-2 contracts and dealing with a potential drawdown on a lower level, then adding later as the analysis plays out.

Perspective 13

(13 EMA vs the 100 EMA zone)
Setup Requires
  • 13 EMA
  • 100 EMA set to high
  • 100 EMA set to low
Directional Bias
Above inclining 100 EMA's — bullish
Below declining 100 EMA's — bearish
Entry & Conditions

Entry: bounce off the inclining or declining 13 EMA

If price and the 13 EMA are trading inside the 100 EMA zone, it's choppy

If the 13 EMA is far away from the 100 EMA zone, a potential reversal can happen

Examples
Bullish
Bounces off the inclining 13 EMA, above the 100 zone
Bearish
Bounces off the declining 13 EMA, below the 100 zone

9MM Strategy

(trending setup)
Setup Requires
  • 5m chart
  • 9 EMA overlay
Concept

Simple 9 EMA bounce on the 5m chart

Price pulls back to the 9 EMA and bounces in the direction of the trend

A bounce off the 5m candle BODY is ideal

Be Aware

At open, or generally, price tends to sweep or wick through the 9 EMA — wait for bounces off the 9 EMA without a sweep or wick, those are the bounces that can be trusted

Another way to see it: if price keeps wicking through, it's making the future move stronger, offering more confidence in the direction

Examples
Long
Pulls back to touch the 9 EMA, bounces up
Short
Pulls back to touch the 9 EMA, bounces down

Break or Sweeps
(market open liquidity concept)

Mark the high/low of the 6:00 AM 4hr candle

Break = price closes beyond the level

Sweep = price wicks beyond the level, closes back inside

The smaller the range, the more likely sweeps can happen on the top and bottom of the range

Also mark the 8:11-9:11 range for break, sweep or bounce

Asia/London and overnight range/levels are also areas for break, sweep or bounce

Break
Closes beyond the 10am level
Sweep
Wicks beyond, closes back inside
2/5 min Protection
(first 2/5m candle as a bounce zone)

Mark the high/low of the first opening 2/5m candle

No trades until price moves under or above the first 2/5m candle

Once the larger range has broken, price tends to return and bounce off this zone
